# Paper

A. Z. Khan, [*Lie Algebra Cohomology and Seiberg-Witten Theory*](Pure_SU2_PVA_Proof.pdf), working
draft dated October 25, 2026 (this PDF built November 1, 2026).

The formalization target is Theorem 1.1. The presentation of the algebra is in Theorem 1.1 and
§2.1, finite polynomial cochains in §2.2, and the cocycles in equation (2.10), §2.3. The paper
writes `z, w` where the Lean documentation writes `x, y`; in both, the second variable is even
and squares to zero.

[SCOPE.md](../docs/SCOPE.md) gives the conventions and what is not formalized.
[DECLARATIONS.md](../docs/DECLARATIONS.md) maps the paper's objects to Lean declarations.
