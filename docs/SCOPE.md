# Statement and scope

The theorem is `KhanSL2.mainStatement : KhanSL2.MainStatement` in
[MainProof.lean](../lean/MainProof.lean). This page states it in mathematical language and
lists the conventions needed to compare it with the paper.

## The theorem

Work over \(\mathbb{C}\). Let \(A\) be the differential supercommutative algebra generated by an
even element \(X\) and an odd element \(Y\) and all their derivatives, modulo the differential
ideal generated by

\[
X^2=XY=X\partial Y=Y\partial Y=0,
\]

with generator brackets

\[
\{X_\lambda X\}=\partial X+2\lambda X,\qquad
\{X_\lambda Y\}=\partial Y+3\lambda Y,\qquad
\{Y_\lambda Y\}=0.
\]

Let \(H\) be the relative Chevalley–Eilenberg cohomology of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2[x,y]/(y^2)\)
relative to the constant \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\), with trivial coefficients, computed from
finite-mode cochains (next section), with its induced product, translation and λ-bracket. Both
\(x\) and \(y\) are even.

In the color basis of [ColorBasis.lean](../lean/KhanSL2/ColorBasis.lean), write \(b_j^a\) for
the coordinate dual to \(t_a x^j y\) and \(c_{j+1}^a\) for the coordinate dual to
\(t_a x^{j+1}\). The cocycle classes are

\[
\mathsf{x}=\left[-\sum_{a=0}^{2}b_0^a\wedge c_1^a\right],\qquad
\mathsf{y}=[b_0^0\wedge b_0^1\wedge b_0^2].
\]

The map

\[
\phi:A\longrightarrow H,\qquad
\partial^iX\longmapsto T^i\mathsf{x},\quad
\partial^iY\longmapsto T^i\mathsf{y}
\]

is a graded differential Poisson vertex superalgebra isomorphism: it is bijective and preserves
the unit, the product, the translation, every λ-bracket coefficient and each homogeneous degree.
`phi` is defined by descending evaluation through the source relations; it is a fixed map, not
one obtained from an existence proof.

## Formal conventions

| Mathematical object or convention | In Lean |
|---|---|
| Ground field | mathlib's `ℂ` |
| Source | `KhanSL2.Source`, a quotient of finite expressions by an explicitly generated relation, closed under translation |
| Cochains | `KhanSL2.Cochain = Finset Mode →₀ ℂ`: finite linear combinations of finite exterior monomials |
| Relative condition | Invariance under each of the three constant color actions |
| Differential | The finite formula `KhanSL2.q`, with proofs that it squares to zero and preserves invariants |
| Cohomology | `KhanSL2.Cohomology`: invariant cycles modulo invariant boundaries |
| Mode indexing | `Mode.c j a` is \(c_{j+1}^a\); the target has no constant \(c_0\) |
| Degree | (total CE degree, y-count, spin); the paper's \((B,p,S)\) is \((B+p,B,S)\) |
| Generator degrees | \(X_i\): \((2,1,i+2)\); \(Y_i\): \((3,3,i+3)\) |
| Translation | Raises spin by one, keeps the other two degrees |
| λ-coefficients | Ordinary powers of λ, with no implicit division by \(n!\) |
| Super signs | Total CE degree modulo two |

`KhanSL2.IsPVA` packages the PVA laws: an internal direct-sum grading, a unital associative
supercommutative product, a translation derivation, sesquilinearity, super skew symmetry, super
Jacobi, super Leibniz and the bracket degree. `source_isPVA` and `target_isPVA` prove them for
the source and the target separately.

## Relation to the paper

References are to the draft of the PDF in `paper/` (dated October 25, 2026; built
November 1, 2026).

- The target is Theorem 1.1. The presentation of \(A\) is in Theorem 1.1 and §2.1; finite
  polynomial cochains and graded duals are in §2.2; the cocycles are equation (2.10) in §2.3.
- The paper writes \(z,w\) for \(x,y\) and numbers the colors 1, 2, 3; Lean numbers them
  0, 1, 2. Degrees convert as in the table.
- Sign: §2.2 gives \([Q,\partial]=-\sum_a c_1^a L_a\). With the color-action convention used in
  Lean, [`q_translation_commutator`](../lean/KhanSL2/CETranslation.lean) proves
  \(QT-TQ=+\sum_a c_1^a L_a\). Both vanish on invariants, so the theorem does not depend on the
  sign.
- Lean proves the factorization step of §6 (`Collision.kFactor`) through its Strong
  Macdonald/Hodge development. The paper's current §6 uses a different argument: cup products
  at disjoint supports and Teleman's theorem (Theorem 3.3).

Lean formalizes the theorem, not the paper's proof line by line.

## Not formalized

- \(H\) always means the finite-mode construction above. Identifying it with the cohomology of
  the full algebraic dual, or of a completed cochain complex, would be a separate theorem.
- The source is a generators-and-relations presentation. No equivalence with mathlib's
  polynomial algebra tensored with an exterior algebra is proved.
- There is no comparison with an external library of relative Lie algebra cohomology.
- The Hilbert series, the constant-term identity, the coherent Satake identification and the
  physical interpretation in the paper.
